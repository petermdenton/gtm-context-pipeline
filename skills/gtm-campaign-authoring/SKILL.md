# Campaign research and creative authoring

Read checkout `AGENTS.md`, the client's offer/claims/brand guidance and the
specific audience brief or cluster snapshot. Resolve requested research depth,
buyer, stage, angle and deliverables. Keep company configuration private; do not
assume an industry, visual style, product claim or fixed creative inventory.

Pin membership, source corpus and context versions. Portal clusters are draft
text components until reviewed for campaign use. Missing captures remain a gap,
not a reproduced source corpus. For fresh collection, read
[source collection](references/source-collection.md) and use workflow discovery
before repetitive website work or a new stage.

Distill task language, problems, alternatives, objections and proof expectations
into a dated draft context. Review excerpts, company/domain identity, source
dates, representativeness and hashes. Audience language supports vocabulary;
another company's claims do not establish the client's performance. Record
claim IDs/sources and qualifications, or mark proposed claims unverified.

Write a buyer/stage/angle argument with a specific task, supported contribution,
proof, limitations and CTA. Use the user's voice and approved company claims.
Keep internal qualification notes separate from outgoing copy. Preserve evidence
when editing; no fabricated quotes, customer outcomes or em dashes.

For creative work, create stable creative IDs and link audience/version, stage,
claims, input assets, destination and review state. Reuse approved artwork when
appropriate. Use available image/video/design tools when actual assets are
requested; a prompt or storyboard is not a rendered asset. Inspect exports at
their intended display size and retain editable sources when produced.

The portal stores cluster-linked briefs, draft creative text and reference
links. It has no creative renderer, media generator, research importer or freeze
CLI. Store detailed research and actual exports in the private run and link them
from the handoff. Use only implemented portal fields if saving drafts; a saved
record does not make an asset approved, rendered or published.

For destinations, continue with [gtm-ad-page](../gtm-ad-page/SKILL.md). For
delivery, hand reviewed assets to
[gtm-campaign-operations](../gtm-campaign-operations/SKILL.md). Deliver actual
files, source trail, versions, review status, checks and gaps. Revise only affected
artifacts when feedback changes a hypothesis. Creative completion does not prove
message resonance or authorize a paid launch.

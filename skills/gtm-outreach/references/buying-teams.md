# Optional buying-team enrichment

Reuse supplied briefs, dated prior research and current professional sources.
Map responsibilities needed for the client's buying decision, not a universal
list of titles. Preserve current title, remit evidence, proposed buying role,
dates and confidence separately. Leave unresolved roles unfilled.

Prefer an existing dedicated connector or user-selected provider. When Monid is
available and useful, read its installed skill, discover relevant endpoints and
inspect current schemas, costs and limit semantics before execution. No provider
or endpoint is required by this localized workflow. Do not assume a historical
people-search API, normalized title vocabulary or price remains valid.

Resolve company identity by domain and corroborating description before people
searches. Filter current employer and relevant responsibilities when supported.
Inspect returned data before purchasing additional profiles; titles and provider
headlines can conflict. Cross-check current employment and remit on primary
sources. Avoid selecting recipients merely because the database has an email.

Use paid calls only within established scope; account for each lookup/page in
the budget and stop at its limit. Wallet balance is not spending authorization.
Do not top up or evade caps by splitting requests. Inspect existing pending runs
before resubmitting; terminal blocked results are not pending work.

Keep requests, response files, run IDs, filters, pagination, source dates and
actual costs privately without secrets. Preserve total, returned and reviewed
counts separately. A full page is not exhaustive coverage, and a missing known
leader does not prove departure. Supplement incomplete sources or record the gap.

Deliver the role map, evidence/inference, selected first contact and specific
verification gaps. Email provenance and deliverability remain separate. Research
completion authorizes no message, CRM write or simultaneous outreach to everyone.

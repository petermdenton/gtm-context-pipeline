# Email drafting and private handoff

Use the user's voice and actual client offer. A concise observation, specific
question/contribution and easy next step often suffice; do not force a fixed
word count, meeting offer or product pitch. Preserve user-supplied wording when
the request is a light edit. Be truthful about the commercial purpose.

The company evidence explains relevance; remit evidence explains the recipient.
Neither alone establishes pain. Distinguish an observed workflow, proposed need,
supported product contribution and unresolved fit. Do not transfer a competitor's
results or one recipient's story to another account. Approved claims and actual
product capability bound promises. Narrow an unsupported use case rather than
hiding the gap with vague copy.

Use a short subject and concrete language. Avoid generic praise, invented
familiarity, architecture summaries, urgency and implied diagnoses. Do not claim
research or a comparison was completed unless it was. Keep citations and internal
notes adjacent to the draft; include only useful links in the outgoing message.

For requested follow-ups, use actual send dates/replies, add a relevant observation
or narrower question, and stop treating the draft as untouched when a reply,
opt-out or referral changes the situation. Unsent copy is not a prior touch.
Suggested timing is not a scheduled message. Do not copy another client's cadence
or silently turn a set of drafts into a sending queue.

A small batch can be one private Markdown file. Add structured data only when a
downstream workflow needs it. Include:

- Company/domain, brief version, source dates, origin batch and selected task.
- Person, exact title, role/remit evidence, sources, dates and identity gaps.
- Buying-team coverage, selected first contact and proposed relationship path.
- Address provenance and what is actually verified; leave unknowns empty.
- Observation, fit hypothesis, supported claim references and unresolved needs.
- Subject/body and message version, separate from research notes.
- Prior outreach, suppression, costs and specific next steps.
- Local draft, provider draft, CRM record and send outcomes separately, with
  verified references and partial failures when those actions were requested.

Check whether the message could be reused unchanged for every other company. If
so, make the observation and requested decision more specific. Uncertain remit
or email remains a research gap, not a send-ready contact. Follow
[mail/CRM guidance](mail-crm.md) only for requested external work.

# Buyer research and outreach

Read checkout `AGENTS.md`, the supplied company brief/version, client offer and
approved claims, selected accounts and relevant prior outreach. Follow the user's
sender, channel, voice and requested depth. This workflow focuses on email;
do not substitute another channel or provider without a reason grounded in the
request. Store named contacts, addresses and personalized drafts privately.

Resolve exact domains and professional company identities. Separate facts from
fit/pain hypotheses. Refresh facts used in messages and current employment/remit
through professional primary sources. Preserve access/publication dates and
conflicts. A senior title, funding round or engagement label does not prove
authority, budget or buying intent. If verification is unavailable, produce
role-targeted drafts with the missing evidence stated outside the message.

## Map the buying team

Use responsibilities appropriate to the client's offer: relevant user/champion,
technical evaluator or implementation owner, business stakeholder and economic
buyer as needed. Do not force every business into an AI engineering team model.
One person can cover several roles; leave unsupported roles unfilled. Preserve
exact current titles separately from proposed buying roles and remit evidence.

Recommend a primary recipient and useful alternatives based on responsibility,
not seniority alone. Keep research proportional: rewriting a supplied email
does not require a new buying-team search. Read
[buying-team research](references/buying-teams.md) for optional enrichment, or
[network handoffs](references/network-handoff.md) when identified engagement or
relationship paths are part of the request.

Business-email provenance and deliverability differ from identity verification.
Use supplied or authorized professional sources; never guess an address from a
naming pattern and call it verified. Reuse valid research before paid enrichment,
which requires established scope. Keep unrelated personal information out.

## Draft and hand off

Read [email guidance](references/email-handoff.md). Default to one initial draft
per selected primary recipient unless a sequence or broader set is requested.
Resolve sequence length/timing from the request or sender profile; if unspecified,
state a reasonable draft-only assumption. Source-project cadence and voice are
not universal client defaults. Missing sender/address fields need not block copy.

Use a specific, truthful observation, a supported contribution and an appropriate
CTA or question. Keep internal source citations and qualification caveats beside
the draft unless the recipient needs them. Do not invent pain, prior contact,
sender experience, customer outcomes or tracking-based personalization. Account
engagement does not prove the recipient clicked. No em dashes.

The portal stores contact records and notes. It does not enrich people, create
mail drafts, enroll sequences or send. Save requested research and copy under
ignored `output/outreach/<run-id>/` or the client's private workspace. Preserve
prior drafts, actual sends, replies, suppression and stable account/person keys.

For requested provider drafts, CRM writes, sending or reconciliation, read
[mail and CRM handoff](references/mail-crm.md). Use the actual supported tool,
schema and account within established scope. Local drafting is not authorization
for those writes. Honor explicit sending authorization already given; prepare
recipient/sender/final content and required checks, then verify the actual result.
On ambiguity, inspect provider evidence before retrying.

Deliver sources, team coverage, selected recipient/remit, drafts, unresolved
fields, costs if incurred and separate local/provider/send states. Preserve
verified replies, meetings and outcomes with source IDs and timestamps; activation
needs product-side evidence. Do not change suppression, campaign membership or
qualification criteria as a side effect of logging an outcome.

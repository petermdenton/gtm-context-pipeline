# Campaign operations and feedback

Read checkout `AGENTS.md`, the client configuration, reviewed batch/taxonomy and
experiment. Determine whether the request is readiness, engagement review,
readout, campaign setup, targeting change or launch. Read
[platform actions and feedback](references/platform-loop.md) for external work.

The toolkit has no campaign sequencer, platform writer or delivery-import CLI.
The portal's Sequences page is an empty state, and ad-account links are not
verified connections. Use actual supported connectors/browser tools within
established scope; distinguish their work from implemented repository features.
Run workflow discovery before planning a repetitive task or new website stage.

Prepare readiness using the actual channel's requirements: owner, audience and
exclusions, buyer/geography filters, matched reach when available, reviewed
assets, verified destination/tracking, currency, budget, schedule and measurement
window. Get these from the request/client, not fixed source-project defaults.
Missing evidence remains a hold. Check current official platform requirements
before relying on a launch or API path.

Use [gtm-analytics](../gtm-analytics/SKILL.md) for aggregate reporting. Preserve
delivery snapshots, account scope, source windows, currency and import/collection
audits. Do not claim a toolkit import ran when only an agent-authored report
exists. Keep identifier-bearing exports private.

Report engagement, fit, exposure and identity confidence separately. Company
counts are not matched reach, and platform labels are not purchase probabilities.
Anonymous site traffic cannot identify an account. Use evidence for actual
replies, meetings and product outcomes; missing outcomes stay null.

At the agreed review cadence, retain budget/suppression holds, stale inputs and
inconclusive samples. Record the reviewer decision and supporting data. A changed
CTR or observational before/after comparison does not establish a winner.

Route fit-qualified discovery hypotheses to [gtm-clusters](../gtm-clusters/SKILL.md),
next-N selection to [gtm-next-companies](../gtm-next-companies/SKILL.md), message
findings to authoring/pages, and identified-account follow-up to
[gtm-outreach](../gtm-outreach/SKILL.md). Only requested buyer/outreach work should
expand an engagement report into contact research.

Deliver concrete plans or verified actions, before/after evidence, gaps and next
decisions. Setup, launch, delivery and sales outcomes are separate states.
Recurring work requires an actual user-requested automation and verified access;
these skills alone do not run a background campaign loop.

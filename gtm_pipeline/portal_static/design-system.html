OUR APPROACH

Clear by design.

Principles that hold the system together
01 / HIERARCHY

Give the work room.

Quiet surfaces, generous space, and one clear primary action per context.

02 / EVIDENCE

Keep the source close.

Pair a number with its meaning, source, and date. Show unknowns as unknowns.

03 / STATE

Say what has happened.

Draft, reviewed, published, and sent describe different events. Keep them distinct.

04 / ACCESS

Make clarity accessible.

Readable contrast, visible focus, useful labels, and layouts that adapt.

Accessibility & motion

Verified token pairs, not a blanket audit.

Focus uses a 2px outline with 3px offset. Touch actions grow to 44px. Reduced motion disables decorative transitions. Keep visible field labels and describe validation errors next to the input.
